How the scoring works
  1. Each of the 3 judges scores 1–10 — enter each round's total (3–30, whole points). The three round totals are summed per couple.
  2. Judge totals are ranked highest → lowest: the highest total earns 5 rank points, the next 4, and so on. Tied totals share the same points and the next total below drops by just 1 — e.g. totals 90, 90, 89, 89, 88 → 5, 5, 4, 4, 3.
  3. You rank the audience vote 1st–5th (no ties): 1st place earns 5 audience points, down to 1 point for 5th.
  4. Judge rank points + audience points are totalled and ranked. A tie on the combined total is broken by the higher audience vote points.
